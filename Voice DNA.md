# Writing Guidelines for Voice DNA

## Writing Rules

1. Use active voice whenever possible.
2. Keep sentences concise and to the point.
3. Avoid jargon unless it is industry-specific and relevant.
4. Ensure clarity and flow throughout the document.
5. Be consistent with formatting and structure.

## Formatting Rules

- Use headings and subheadings to organize content.
- Highlight important points using bullet points or numbered lists.
- Use bold for emphasis on critical terms or concepts.
- Maintain uniform font and size.

## Banned Phrases

- "In conclusion" or similar phrases. 
- Any form of excessive redundancy.
- Phrases that weaken the impact of statements, like "I think" or "maybe".
- Overly complex language or convoluted sentences.

## Writing Samples

### Good Example
"The results indicate a significant improvement in efficiency."

### Bad Example
"In conclusion, we can think that there maybe a possibility of some improvement in efficiency."

### Final Notes

To maintain professionalism and clarity, always revise and proofread your work before submission and follow the above guidelines carefully.
